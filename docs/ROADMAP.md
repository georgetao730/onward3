# Onward3 — Learning Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundations

### 01. What is Web3?
Understand what changes when identity, assets, and application state can live on decentralized networks.

### 02. Blockchain
Blocks, transactions, consensus, state, and why append-only shared ledgers exist.

### 03. Bitcoin
UTXO, mining, digital scarcity, and Bitcoin's role in the history of Web3.

### 04. Ethereum
A programmable blockchain, accounts, state, and the idea of a global execution environment.

### 05. Accounts & Wallets
EOAs, wallet software, addresses, and why a wallet is not the same thing as an account.

### 06. Private Keys & Public Keys
Key generation, signatures, addresses, ownership, and security mental models.

### 07. Transactions
Nonce, value, calldata, signatures, transaction lifecycle, and confirmation.

### 08. Gas
Why computation costs money, gas units, gas price, fees, and execution limits.

### 09. Smart Contracts
Deterministic programs deployed to blockchain state and how they differ from Web2 backend services.

### 10. EVM
Bytecode, opcodes, storage, memory, calldata, execution, and EVM vs JVM mental models.
